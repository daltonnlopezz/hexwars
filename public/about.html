About WARFORGE

WARFORGE: Realms at War is a hex-based strategy game for up to six players. Each player leads one faction, the Vanguard, the Empire, the Ironborne, the Raiders, the HORDE or the Damned, and fights for the land through conquest, trade, alliances and betrayal.

From the table to the screen

WARFORGE started as a physical board game: a hex grid drawn on graph paper, tiles modelled and 3D printed, and rules tested over many games on the table. The digital version is built on those same rules, so the game you play on screen is the game that was played on the table.

The factions

Every faction plays differently. Some win through reputation, trade and diplomacy; others, like the HORDE and the Damned, only through conquest. Learn more on the Factions page.

Made by people

The game's core rules were designed by hand for the tabletop game. The final art, models, music and sound are made by human artists, and we commission artists directly. If you're an artist interested in working on WARFORGE, send us a message on Discord or Instagram.

Follow development

Every update gets a dev diary. Playtests and discussion happen on Discord, and you can follow along on Instagram and X.
