Game Tools Overview

Explore WARFORGE tools for planning battles, managing kingdoms, and researching factions. Each tool below includes a quick overview and how-to.

Battle Calculator

Simulate battles between factions with configurable units, leaders, terrain, and buildings. View winner, power breakdowns, losses, and special results.

How to Use
  • Select factions and populate unit counts.
  • Choose origin tile, battle tile, and buildings.
  • Optional: add supporters and leaders.
  • Click Calculate to view detailed results.

Resource Calculator

Plan income, expenses, buildings, and terrain benefits to keep your kingdom thriving.

How to Use
  • Enter resource pools and terrain/building counts.
  • Adjust unit recruitment and upkeep.
  • Use Calculate to see per-round breakdowns.
