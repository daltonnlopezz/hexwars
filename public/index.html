Realms at War

Welcome to WARFORGE
WARFORGE: Realms at War is a hex-based strategy game for up to six players. It started as a physical board game and is now being built as a digital game. Lead one of six factions, the Vanguard, the Empire, the Ironborne, the Raiders, the HORDE or the Damned, and claim land, build, trade, make and break alliances, and go to war.
