Welcome to HEX Wars
HEX Wars is a growing universe with stories, games, and systems evolving together. I’m building an interconnected world of factions, characters, and conflicts across multiple formats: tabletop/board games, written stories, and web experiences. This hub introduces the universe, links to playable tools, and tracks progress across lore, game design, and programming.
